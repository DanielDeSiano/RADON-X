From volcanic soil to a testable model

Daniel De Siano developed RADON-X to investigate how radon moves through volcanic soil. The simulator combines radon production, radioactive decay and diffusion through a soil column. An optional indoor compartment estimates how the simulated soil source can affect indoor concentration.

The Ischia preset provides an exploratory starting point. The archive documents the origin and intended use of the datasets included with the project.
